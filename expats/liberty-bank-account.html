Opening a Liberty Bank Account: Start Before You Arrive

Liberty is Georgia's third major bank, with the widest branch network in the country, straightforward products and lower fees than the two giants. For everyday banking — or as a smart second account — it's an underrated choice. Prepared properly, your account opens in a single one-hour branch visit with a private banker we work with.

Why Liberty Bank

Liberty doesn't compete with Bank of Georgia and TBC on lounges and premium tiers — it competes on reach and price. It operates the largest branch network in Georgia (it handles the state pension system, so there's a Liberty in practically every town), its fees are generally lower, and its mobile app is modern and functional. For straightforward personal banking, Liberty's process can also feel lighter than the big two.

Where you'll notice the difference is English: support is adequate but less consistent than at Bank of Georgia or TBC. This is exactly why our private-banker relationship matters — you'll deal with a named, English-speaking contact rather than taking your chances at a branch counter.

Many of our clients open Liberty as a second account alongside BOG or TBC: it costs little, diversifies where your money sits, and is a lifesaver if the primary bank's compliance ever freezes a transfer for review.

What you can do before you arrive

Liberty has no online application for non-residents, so the remote preparation runs entirely through the document pack and our banker contact — which, in practice, achieves the same result:

  1. Assemble your document pack. Scan everything in the checklist below as clear colour PDFs or photos.
  2. Write down your KYC answers. One or two sentences each: why Georgia, what the account is for, where your money comes from, and a realistic estimate of monthly turnover.
  3. Send the pack to Tbilisi Home. We forward it to our private banker at Liberty, who pre-reviews the file and tells us in advance if compliance will want anything more.
  4. Tell us your arrival date. We book your branch appointment with the private banker for a day soon after you land.
  5. On arrival: Georgian SIM, then the bank. A local number is needed for SMS codes; we help you get a SIM within an hour of landing. Then one visit: original passport, signatures, account opened, card ordered.

Document checklist

  • Passport — colour scan of the photo page now; the original at the branch.
  • Bank statements — the last 6–12 months from your main existing bank account.
  • Proof of income or wealth — whichever applies: employment contract or employer letter, pension statement, company ownership documents, tax return, investment statement, or a property/business sale contract.
  • Purpose evidence (if buying property) — your preliminary purchase agreement or the details of the apartment you're buying through us.
  • Georgian phone number — obtained on arrival (Magti, Silknet or Cellfie; a passport is all you need for a SIM).
Compliance notes: Liberty follows the same international KYC rules as every Georgian bank. Specific, honest answers about income and purpose keep things moving; politically exposed persons should declare it upfront; undocumented cryptocurrency income is a common reason files stall. Straightforward cases are frequently approved same-day; non-resident reviews can take a few business days.

The branch visit — what actually happens

  • The banker verifies your original passport and takes your signatures on the application and KYC forms.
  • You confirm your prepared answers on income, purpose and expected turnover.
  • Your accounts (GEL, plus USD/EUR as needed) are opened, pending any remaining compliance review.
  • You choose and order your card — often issued at the branch or within a few working days, with lower issuance and service fees than the premium banks.
  • The banker sets up Liberty's mobile app and internet banking on your phone.
  • Any one-time fees are modest — Liberty is usually the cheapest of the three to open and run.

Can I open it without coming to Georgia at all?

Yes — like the other Georgian banks, Liberty accepts openings through a representative holding a notarised (and usually apostilled) power of attorney and a notarised copy of your passport. Tbilisi Home can connect you with a trusted lawyer to act as your representative and coordinate with our Liberty contact.

Allow roughly 2–4 weeks including document couriering, and expect stricter compliance checks than an in-person opening. For anyone travelling to Georgia anyway, the prepare-remotely-plus-one-visit route is faster and cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a residence permit or visa?

No — a valid passport is enough; Georgia has no residency requirement for personal accounts. Citizens of most countries can stay visa-free for a full year.

Is Liberty safe? It's smaller than BOG and TBC.

Liberty is a systemically important, licensed Georgian bank supervised by the National Bank of Georgia, and it operates the country's pension payment infrastructure. Smaller than the big two, yes — but a long-established, regulated institution.

Should Liberty be my main bank or my second bank?

If low fees and simple everyday banking are your priority, it works well as a main account. Most of our expat clients, though, use Liberty as a second account alongside Bank of Georgia or TBC — best of both worlds: premium service at one bank, cheap redundancy at the other.

What can go wrong?

The same things as anywhere: undocumented income, vague answers, or a higher-risk profile triggering enhanced due diligence. Approval is always the bank's decision — but pre-review catches most problems before you fly, and with contacts at all three banks we can quickly pivot if one says no.
